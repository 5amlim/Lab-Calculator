# v11.67: Submission-bag specimen badges

- Added specimen identity badges at the far right of each transport item title.
- Color groups: stool (tan), urine (yellow), serum (green), plasma (blue), whole blood (rose), RBCs (red), swab (lavender), other/verify (gray). Mixed-source cards display MIXED SPECIMENS rather than an inaccurate single type.
- Source processing instructions and transfer-source badges remain on the line directly beneath the item heading, unchanged.
- Directory-wide QA: 8,072 checks confirmed identical bag plan, quantities, and source line for every active test.
- Chromium print QA: 390px and 1280px viewports both produced 4 pages for a representative serum/stool/plasma/whole blood order. PDF text and card positions matched except for the generated timestamp.
- iPhone Safari native print dialog was not tested.
- No calculations, specimen labeling, or submission rules changed.
