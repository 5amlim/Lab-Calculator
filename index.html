Order of draw for nurses

Order-of-draw reference ↗
Butterfly collection: If a light-blue citrate tube is the first specimen, draw a partially filled citrate discard tube first to fill the tubing dead space, then fill the test tube completely.
Important: Confirm the additive on the tube label. Do not rely on stopper color alone. This sequence applies when collecting multiple blood tubes; follow test-specific official instructions and facility policy.
