Version 6.5

- Corrected SST collection-tube estimation so a preferred volume alone cannot make one test count as more than one SST.
- Multiple SSTs are now counted for one test only when the collection instructions explicitly require multiple, dedicated, separate, or full SST tubes.
- For test codes 498, 8472, and 91431 together, the calculator now estimates no more than 3 SST tubes.
- Test codes 17169 and 36126 each contribute no more than one SST unless explicit instructions are later added.
- Transport tubes and aliquots remain counted separately in the submission plan.
- All v6.4 fasting alerts, collection, bag, ownership, and agreement features remain unchanged.
