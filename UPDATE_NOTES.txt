v10.54

- Restored the What to collect layout to one continuous compact grid instead of breaking the grid into separate Urine/Stool rows.
- Added a small collection-type badge inside non-blood cards: Random urine, First-morning urine, Timed urine, 24-hour urine, or Stool.
- Compatible tests collected from the same urine specimen and same physical collection container remain grouped; urine collections with materially different collection types remain separate.
- 24-hour urine submission items now show a label instruction in the transport bag: “24h urine · Total volume: ____ mL · Start: ____ · End: ____”.
- No test catalog data, blood pooling, draw-volume calculations, Do Not Perform status, or storage architecture changed.

v10.53

- Urine tests collected from the same Sterile Urine Cup now appear together in one What to collect card, even if they are transferred into different downstream preservative or transport tubes.
- Urine and stool remain separated even when both use the physical Sterile Urine Cup.
- Spot-urine cup counts remain pooled as one source cup unless an explicit larger collection count is required.
- No test data, blood pooling, draw-volume calculations, submission routing, Do Not Perform status, or storage architecture changed.
