Version 6.6

Fasting preparation audit

- Reviewed all 257 built-in test records for fasting requirements or recommendations.
- Added explicit Fasting required or Fasting preferred data to 27 verified records.
- Fasting alerts identify the affected test code, test name, and preparation instruction in the selected-test summary and printout.
- Added fasting flags for tests including C-peptide, insulin, iron studies, metabolic panels, NMR testing, CTx, selected micronutrients, selected fatty-acid/cardiometabolic tests, the large ANA reflex panel, and other verified specialty tests.
- Standard lipid panels remain unflagged because their current directory instructions do not require fasting when ordered as a lipid panel.
- Improved the fasting detector to prioritize verified structured data and recognize additional fasting wording.
- Requirements can vary by test version and service area; staff should verify unusual or changed orders in the current official directory.
