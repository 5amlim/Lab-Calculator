- Increased the browser database version while preserving custom staff entries.

Version 7.9
- Added system-wide pooling for compatible Lavender EDTA and Red Top collection tubes.
- Lavender whole-blood submissions are calculated separately from Lavender tubes that must be centrifuged for plasma or RBC preparation.
- Red Top serum source tubes pool only within compatible processing and transport-temperature groups.
- Original-tube submissions and source tubes for transferred specimens remain separate in both collection and submission counts.
- One test cannot generate more than one tube of the same kind from volume alone; extra same-kind tubes require an explicit multiple-tube, dedicated-tube, or full-tube instruction.
- Improved recognition of explicit multiple SST instructions, including spun Microtainer and sized SST wording, without treating multiple transport aliquots as multiple phlebotomy tubes.
- No built-in test requirements or order-of-draw rules changed.

Version 7.8
- Corrected SST collection counting when a selection mixes original-SST submissions with serum/plasma transfers from SST.
- Original-submit SSTs and source-for-transfer SSTs are now estimated separately, preventing transferred specimens from borrowing capacity from tubes that must remain in the submission bag.
- Example: 34429 + 58984 + 7260 + 10124 + 809 now plans 3 Gold/SST plus 1 Lavender EDTA.
- No test data or order-of-draw rules changed.
