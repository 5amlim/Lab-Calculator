QUEST LAB CALCULATOR v4.1

- Fixed the custom draw-container workflow.
- The manual text field is now always visible directly below the Draw container dropdown.
- Typing in the manual field automatically selects “Other / manually type.”
- The field becomes required when Other is selected.
- All other calculator features remain unchanged.

Quest Lab Calculator v4.0

- Renamed the final Draw container option to “Other / manually type.”
- Selecting it reveals a required text field for any uncommon tube, cup, swab, or collection kit.
- Existing custom container values remain editable.
- All other calculator behavior remains unchanged.

VERSION 3.9

- Changed Draw container in Add missing test/Edit test to a dropdown.
- Added standard blood, urine, stool, swab, and specialty collection-container options.
- Added Other / not listed for uncommon containers while preserving existing custom values.
- All v3.8 collection, processing, bag, print, and tube-badge behavior remains unchanged.

VERSION 3.8

- In “What to submit after processing,” specimens kept in their original collection tube retain the same tube badge used in “What to collect.”
- Original-tube submissions now state the specimen and clearly say “Submit in original tube.”
- Example: Arsenic, Blood displays Royal Blue EDTA with “Whole Blood · Submit in original tube.”
- Spun SST specimens submitted in the gel tube display the Gold/SST badge with “Serum · spun · submit in original tube.”
- Transferred serum, plasma, and RBC specimens continue to display a transport-tube badge and their source tube.
- All v3.7 collection, urine, transport-bag, search, and print features remain unchanged.

VERSION 3.7

- Added a dedicated red/yellow swirl-top urinalysis preservative tube badge.
- Added a separate sterile urine cup badge.
- Spot urine orders now automatically include one sterile urine cup for initial collection.
- The printout now separates TOTAL TO COLLECT from TOTAL TO SUBMIT.
- TOTAL TO COLLECT counts blood tubes and patient collection containers, including the temperature-aware SST calculation.
- TOTAL TO SUBMIT counts transport bags by temperature and lists processed contents inside each bag.
- Every collection and submission item identifies the Quest tests it supports.
- UA with reflex to culture now shows sterile cup collection, then separate red/yellow swirl UA and gray-top urine culture preservative tubes for submission.
- Urine devices remain excluded from the blood order of draw.
