v10.74
- Changed blood source-for-transfer counting to favor simpler processing over maximum tube sharing.
- Every blood-transfer From pill now always shows a source-tube number.
- Blood source tubes are pooled within each destination submission card, but are no longer shared across different destination cards.
- What to Collect now intentionally draws the sum of the numbered blood source pills, so the blood transfer counts reconcile exactly.
- Example: if one Serum Transport Tube card shows 2 From Gold / SST and one Amber Serum Transport Tube card shows 1 From Gold / SST, What to Collect includes 3 source-for-transfer SSTs for those cards.
- Original-submit tubes remain separate and are added on top of the source-for-transfer total.
- Non-blood collection behavior is unchanged.

v10.73
- Blood source-count pills were allocated across destination cards to match the pooled source-for-transfer draw total.
