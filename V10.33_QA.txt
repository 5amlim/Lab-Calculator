Primary defect corrected
- Free-text gram quantities no longer become collection-container counts.
- 14628 and 5022 each calculate 1 Stool Container, not 10.
- 1304 calculates 1 Sterile Urine Cup, not 5.

Cross-specimen count safeguards
- Hours/minutes, RCF/RPM values, tube dimensions, K2 additive text, test codes, and patient-identifier counts do not change collection counts.
- Submission counts are limited to structured transport counts or explicit “Submit N ...” language.

Pooling
- SST, Lavender EDTA, and Red Top pooling retained.
- Added compatible pooling for light-blue citrate, heparin/green, pink, tan, royal-blue variants, gray, and ACD blood tubes.
- Pooling remains separated by exact collection-container identity, specimen material/path, and transport-temperature bag.
- Explicit dedicated/multiple collection requirements still override pooling.
