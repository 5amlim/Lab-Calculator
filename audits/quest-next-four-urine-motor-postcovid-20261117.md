Original collection tubes and submitted transfer vessels are different physical items where applicable. Printed specimen labels retain **SERUM / URINE / RANDOM URINE / 24-HOUR URINE** identity. Draw versus submit grouping uses explicit additional requirements for multi-material panels. Standard clinic Gold SST usable serum yield = 2.5 mL; Tiger = 4 mL. Room-temperature and refrigerated bags remain separate.
