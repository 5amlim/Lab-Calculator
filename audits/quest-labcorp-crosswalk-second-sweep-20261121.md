# Second Quest-to-Labcorp crosswalk sweep (2026-11-21)

## Scope and totals

- Compared all 2,692 Quest-directory source rows (2,691 Quest codes and one uncoded 24-hour urine reference) with the 6,015-record Labcorp search index.
- 15 additional workbook-only rows remain; with the uncoded Quest-directory source reference there are 16 entries lacking Quest codes.
- Previous crosswalk: 593 possible matches across 2,707 total rows.
- Second pass adds 237 individually selected analyte/specimen candidate pairs and 34 single-analyte IgG/IgG4 serum candidates, **271 new matches**.
- Updated crosswalk: 855 Quest-coded entries plus 9 workbook-only entries with possible Labcorp matches, **864 total**.
- 1,836 Quest-coded entries remain unmapped, plus 1 uncoded directory source row.
- Refined Quest 8181 (A1c with calculated mean plasma glucose) from Labcorp 001453 (HbA1c only) to Labcorp 102525 (HbA1c with eAG).

## Evidence and exclusions

The Labcorp searchable catalog and official test pages were used as cross-reference resources, not as proof of clinical equivalence. Examples checked on Labcorp official pages:
- Ethosuximide, serum/plasma: https://www.labcorp.com/tests/007443/ethosuximide-serum-or-plasma
- Multi-specimen growth-hormone codes: https://www.labcorp.com/test-menu/resources/multiple-specimen-testing
- Timothy grass specific IgE: https://www.labcorp.com/tests/602506/timothy-grass
- A1c with eAG: https://www.labcorp.com/tests/102525/hemoglobin-hb-a1c-with-eag-whe
- Primidone with phenobarbital measured: https://www.labcorp.com/tests/007856/primidone-serum-or-plasma

Candidates were restricted to matching principal specimen classes. Additional red flags were excluded: IgE vs IgG substitutions, serum vs plasma when not explicitly supported, free vs total measurements, single analytes vs broader panels, nonmatching reflex algorithms, and mismatched species or allergen sources. Timed specimen counts and antibody classes were kept distinct.

**All entries are suggestions only.** Before using a cross-reference for clinical ordering, confirm analyte, analytical method, reporting components, specimen requirements, handling, active Labcorp order status, service area, and receiving lab capabilities. No collection plan, bag calculation, transport rule, Do Not Perform status, or active Quest/Labcorp test was modified by this crosswalk update.
