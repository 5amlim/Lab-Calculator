# Labcorp final 151-reference feasibility audit, November 21, 2026

**Scope:** 151 references left directory-only after the preceding 273-case disposition. Each of the 151 was reviewed; only exact-code verified, collection-safe routes were activated.

- **9 activated** with a collection route, specimen-specific label, temperature, volume, category and source reference.
- **7 moved to Do Not Perform:** six acid-preservative or specialty urine collection exclusions and a discontinued MLH1 code.
- **135 remain directory-only** rather than being made cart-ready from unverified kit availability, orderability, unsupported preparatory steps, missing exact-code instructions or source contradictions.
- **No unconfirmed turnaround time is entered.** Stillbirth profile code 365200 receives official 2–3-day TAT; all other new entries have no verified exact-code TAT.
